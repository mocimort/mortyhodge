About

Morty Hodge founded the company that became Hodge Industrial Technologies in January 2010. What started as a one-man compressed air service operation in Georgia has grown into a multi-state platform serving industrial customers across the United States, built on a 96% uptime guarantee and roughly two-hour emergency response times.

Hodge Compressor, the company's manufacturing arm, builds and supports its own line of rotary screw air compressors and air treatment equipment, backed by a lifetime airend warranty.

Before founding the company, Morty worked in the compressed air industry and holds FAA Airframe & Powerplant mechanic licenses from an FAA-approved program at MIAT College of Technology.

Writing & Speaking

Morty is the author of The Lost Sale Autopsy, a book documenting the sales methodology behind scaling his company's sales 15X. He speaks and appears on podcasts covering leadership, sales, and building service businesses, including the Speak with People podcast.

Community

Morty serves on the board of Little Red Acorn, a nonprofit that provides scholarships to people entering the skilled trades, in partnership with the Trades Education Center in Arcade, Georgia. He is a member of the Georgia Manufacturing Alliance.
